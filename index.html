The fold

One clean rectangle, three creases, eight layers. Each layer keeps a fixed teal face up and a fixed coral face down — folding never swaps them.

The honest bit

The 8-fold is compact and it does keep the dirty side sealed inside — but to get past the first two surfaces you have to open the cloth again, which re-exposes what you already wiped. Watch the used faces on the table counter climb as you open. A roll fold avoids this entirely: one continuous pass, 100% of the area, nothing ever re-exposed.
